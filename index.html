FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Do I need to supply water or power?

No — our rig is fully self-sufficient. We bring our own water tank, generator and hot-water extraction equipment. All we need is space to park beside your vehicle.

What if it rains on my booking day?

We work rain or shine. If the weather is truly severe we'll contact you to rearrange — you'll never be left guessing.

How long does a detail take?

A hand wash takes about an hour. Interior details typically run two to four hours depending on the level and the state of the vehicle. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm your booking.

Why are prices listed "for sedans"?

Bigger vehicles take more time and product. Listed prices are exact for standard sedans, coupes and hatchbacks; SUVs, trucks and vans are quoted by size and condition when we confirm your booking — before any work starts.

Do I have to be home during the detail?

No. As long as we can access the vehicle and park next to it, you're free to head out — most customers hand us the keys and get on with their day.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged online. You pay when the job is done and you've walked around the car — cash or e-transfer both work.

Can you remove pet hair, salt stains or odours?

Yes — that's our bread and butter. Pet hair and stain removal are part of our interior details, and heavily soiled vehicles just carry a surcharge that we agree with you up front.
